## Deliberately absent

There are no copied vendor blobs, copied stock overlays, fake properties,
placeholder HALs, mass sepolicy rules, permissive policy, or fabricated kernel
settings. No `system.prop` exists because genuine Android 17/SDK37 properties
are generated by the product. In particular, `ro.adb.secure=0` is absent.

No boot/kernel/vendor/DLKM make rule is supplied. The product sets their build
flags false and `TARGET_NO_KERNEL=true`, preserving the currently captured
Ulefone kernel/vendor strategy rather than pretending to reproduce it.
