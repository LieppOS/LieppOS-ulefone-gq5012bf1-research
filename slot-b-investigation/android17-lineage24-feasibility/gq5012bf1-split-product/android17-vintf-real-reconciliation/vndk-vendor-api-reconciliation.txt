## 5. vendor target-level=8 and vendor API level 34 under Android 17
 - Android 17 still ships compatibility_matrix.7.xml and .8.xml (hardware/interfaces/compatibility_matrices/Android.bp SYSTEM_MATRIX_DEPS_A17).
   FCM 7 is the oldest supported => target-level 8 is inside the supported window. libvintf combines 8 + 202404/202504/202604(optional).
 - Framework manifest HALs with max-level="8" (hidl.manager, hidl.token, hidl.memory, sensorservice@1.0, media.c2@1.2 software)
   remain advertised for a level-8 device; max-level="7" HALs (netd@1.1, wifi.keystore, cameraservice@2.2) are dropped (not needed by FCM 8).
 - ro.vendor.api_level=34 (== ro.board.first_api_level=34): the property is NOT consulted by checkvintf 17 (only apex.all.ready,
   ro.boot.product.hardware.sku, ro.boot.product.vendor.sku were queried; see CHECKVINTF-RESULT.txt). Its compatibility is expressed
   via <vendor-ndk>34 (PASS), sepolicy 34.0 (PASS) and target-level 8 (PASS).
 - ro.product.first_api_level=35 likewise not consulted by checkvintf (kernel level comes from the GKI release / target-level).
