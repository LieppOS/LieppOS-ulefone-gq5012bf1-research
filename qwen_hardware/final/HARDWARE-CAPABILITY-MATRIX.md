# Three-layer hardware capability matrix

**Revised 2026-09-08** after the second-pass verification (`../verification/`).

`CHIP CAN DO` = silicon ceiling; `BOARD SUPPORTS` = fitted and configured wiring/drivers; `STOCK USES` = what Ulefone's own software does. A tick in one column never implies a tick in the next.

> **Critical caveat on the third column.** The only live capture available was taken on a device running a **LieppOS GSI** over stock vendor/kernel. Framework-level behaviour in that capture is **not** stock Ulefone behaviour. Cells marked *(GSI)* were observed under the GSI; cells marked *(stock image)* were read from the extracted stock partitions; genuine stock **runtime** behaviour has never been measured.

## Reading rule

The genuinely fitted-but-unused or under-exposed hardware is short and specific: the **DW9781C path** (lens ownership unknown, HAL exposes OFF only), **camera high-rate/HDR modes**, **spare flash/IR channels**, **AW2013 pattern engine**, **sensor die rates above HAL caps**, **dual-band GNSS**, and the **direct-charge stack**. Several items that previous revisions treated as latent capability - wireless charging, UFCS, alternate RF front-end modules, rich haptics - **do not exist on this board** and must not be promised. No column may be upgraded from a marketing specification alone.
