## Dependency-aware execution

Retain requested Mali → APUSYS → camera → VCodec → MDP → auxiliary priority. First reuse the already DONE GKI, mailbox/IPI, secure-memory and display providers and freeze GPUEB/VCP headers. Aux host parity can be advanced in parallel or before its blocked consumers if source-header/CRC tests require it; no need to delay Mali discovery or repeat inventory. Camera starts with PDA/VMM residuals and full-module acceptance, **not** a rewrite of 16 published function/data proof pairs.
