## Evidence strength behind the classes

| strength | meaning | compatibles |
|---|---|---:|
| `pack-claims` | a DONE/PARTIAL pack quotes the compatible in its own README/notes/evidence | 96 |

| `family` | platform-internals family with a written justification | 258 |
| `curated` | audit override (GPU gap, TEE OS instances) | 5 |

Research-doc ownership is the weakest of the "documented" tiers: it proves the node was
examined during an investigation, not that a component pack maintains it. The widest
research owners are `research:reverse-engineering` (68), `research:kernel` (48),
`research:workspace` (45) and `research:qwen_hardware` (43); the widest pack owners are
`MTU3-SSUSB`/`USB-PHY` (18 each, both from `live-dt-usb-nodes.txt`), `GZ-TRANSPORT` (20 in
`phase4-gz-transport-dt-contract.md`) and `DTB-DTBO` (19).

## Conclusions for this axis

1. **No device-tree gap except one.** 577 of 580 compatibles resolve to a documented or
   justified class. The three `E` rows are one subsystem: `arm,mali-valhall`,
   `mediatek,mali` and `mediatek,g3d_brisket` (+ the `gpu_qos`/`gpu_fdvfs` perf nodes).
   The DT axis therefore independently confirms the kernel axis: **the Mali Bifrost/Valhall
   GPU stack has no owning component**. This is the only true coverage hole found so far
   and it needs a decision (dedicated `GPU-KERNEL` component, or an explicit class-C record
   saying "vendor DDK + KMD carried as-is").
2. **The DT axis and module axis agree on the charging stack.** The PMIC charging sub-nodes
   (`mediatek,charger,{pe,pe2,pe45,pe5p,hvbp}`) resolve to `MT6375-CHARGER` only through a
   family rule; no pack quotes them. Same symptom as conclusion 4 of the kernel axis: the
   charging stack is covered by six packs and by none of them as a whole.
3. **`DTB-DTBO` (PARTIAL) does not inventory the tree it owns.** It quotes 19 of 580
   compatibles; everything else is claimed by whichever investigation pack happened to need
   that node, or falls into the platform-internals families. The reproducible artifact
   exists and is good (merged DTS + dtbo table + dtc logs), but node-level ownership lives
   in nobody's inventory. That matches the pack's own "Still required" line ("source-complete
   DTS and physical validation for every node") and is the natural home for this table.
4. **The display and camera pipes are carried, not documented.** `MEDIATEK-DRM` owns 50
   display-pipeline compatibles and `CAMERA-HAL-ISP` 37 camera-pipe compatibles as class B/C:
   they travel with the vendor DDI/mtkcam drivers. That is the right call for a same-BSP
   port, but it must stay an explicit decision: a custom display path (or any ISP change)
   would turn each of those rows into real work, which is exactly why they are recorded
   per row rather than summarised away.
5. **Sensors/TUI/APU IOMMU banks are only covered by research notes.** 18 compatibles
   (`mediatek,smi-common`, `mediatek,common-{apu,disp}-iommu*-bank*`, `mediatek,tui_common`,
   `mediatek,mm_vpu_m*_sub_common`, ...) are owned by `research:sensors` - i.e. documented
   inside phase-4 investigation notes with no pack inventory row.
6. **Legacy/inert payload is tiny.** Only `mediatek,geniezone-hyp` is disabled-as-a-class;
   the DTB carries no dead board variants worth stripping, unlike the module axis (14 D
   modules).
