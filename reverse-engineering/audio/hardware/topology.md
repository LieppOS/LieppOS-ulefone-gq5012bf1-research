## Supplies and ownership

MT6369 is an SPMI PMIC, not I2C. The SPMI PMIC MFD owns its regmap and IRQ
controller; `snd-soc-mt6369` consumes that regmap. `mt6369-regulator` exposes
PMIC rails; microphone bias is generated by MT6369's audio block, not VANT18.

The AW88394 kernel module deliberately does not register its own I2C driver.
`mtk-sp-spk-amp.ko` owns the `mediatek,speaker_amp` node and calls the exported
AW883XX probe/remove functions.
